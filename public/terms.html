Program name

sunday. — Weekly Meal Planner

Description

sunday. is an AI-powered meal planning service that sends weekly meal suggestions via SMS/MMS and adds selected groceries to your Kroger cart.

Messaging terms

  • Message frequency: Approximately 2-4 messages per week (meal suggestions + cart confirmations).
  • Message and data rates may apply. Contact your carrier for details.
  • To opt out: Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe immediately.
  • To get help: Reply HELP to any message for support information.

Service

sunday. uses AI (Google Gemini) to generate meal suggestions and food photography. Meal suggestions are generated recommendations, not dietary or medical advice. The service adds items to your Kroger cart but does not place orders or process payments — you complete checkout directly through Kroger.

Kroger integration

By connecting your Kroger account, you authorize sunday. to search for products and add items to your Kroger cart on your behalf. You can disconnect your account at any time through the app. sunday. is not affiliated with The Kroger Co.

Availability

The service is provided as-is. We do not guarantee uninterrupted availability. Meal generation depends on third-party AI services and Kroger API availability.

Contact

Questions or support: open an issue or reach out via the app.
